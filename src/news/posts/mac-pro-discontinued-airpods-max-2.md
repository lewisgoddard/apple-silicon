title: "Mac Pro discontinued; AirPods Max 2 arrives with H2"
date: 2026-03-31
summary: "The Mac Pro leaves the lineup having never moved past M2 Ultra, and the AirPods Max finally gets an H2 successor."
categories: ["mac/mac-pro", "airpods/max/2", "h2", "m2-ultra"]
---

The [Mac Pro](/devices/mac/mac-pro/) has been discontinued. It shipped with the [M2 Ultra](/chips/m2/ultra/) in both its configurations — [60c CPU / 60c GPU](/chips/m2/ultra/60-60/) and [76c CPU / 76c GPU](/chips/m2/ultra/76-76/) — and never received another chip, so its entire Apple silicon life was a single generation.

Separately, the [AirPods Max 2](/devices/airpods/max/2/) replaces the original [AirPods Max](/devices/airpods/max/1/), moving from [H1](/chips/h1/) to [H2](/chips/h2/). The first-generation model is now discontinued.

That leaves the H2 in six products: the AirPods Max 2, [AirPods Pro 3](/devices/airpods/pro/3/), [AirPods Pro 2](/devices/airpods/pro/2/), [AirPods 4](/devices/airpods/base/4/), [AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation](/devices/airpods/with-active-noise-cancellation/4/) and [Apple Vision Pro](/devices/vision/apple-vision-pro/).
