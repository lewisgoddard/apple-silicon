title: "Mac Studio M3 Ultra drops its 512 GB memory option"
date: 2026-03-16
summary: "The Mac Studio's top chip loses its largest configuration, leaving 96 and 256 GB against an unchanged 819.2 GB/s."
categories: ["m3-ultra", "m3-ultra-32-80", "m3-ultra-28-60", "mac/mac-studio"]
---

Due to ongoing RAM shortages attributed to the AI boom, the [M3 Ultra](/chips/m3/ultra/) in the [Mac Studio](/devices/mac/mac-studio/) no longer offers 512 GB of memory. Both the 28c/60c and 32c/80c parts now top out at 256 GB, with a base configuration of 96 GB.

All RAM options for the M3 Ultra Mac Studio feature a 1024-bit and 819.2 GB/s of bandwidth.
