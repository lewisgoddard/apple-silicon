The [iPhone 17e](/devices/iphone/17/e/) arrived today with a [6c CPU / 4c GPU A19](/chips/a19/base/6-4/). It is the new smallest member of the [A19 family](/chips/a19/), as the [iPhone 17](/devices/iphone/17/base/) uses a [6c CPU / 5c GPU A19](/chips/a19/base/6-5/).

|                        | CPU     | GPU     | Neural Engine | Memory | Bandwidth |
| ---------------------- | ------- | ------- | ------------- | ------ | --------- |
| A19 6c/5c (iPhone 17)  | 6 cores | 5 cores | 16 cores      | 8 GB   | 68.3 GB/s |
| A19 6c/4c (iPhone 17e) | 6 cores | 4 cores | 16 cores      | 8 GB   | 68.3 GB/s |

Both are manufactured on TSMC's 3nm node: One GPU core is the sole difference on the SoC — the 17e keeps the same CPU, Neural Engine, and 8 GB of RAM at the same bandwidth.

### New modem, no new radios

The 17e ships with the [C1X](/chips/c1/x/), Apple's 4nm second-generation modem, described on this site as twice the speed of the C1. That is a step up from the [iPhone 16e](/devices/iphone/16/e/), which used the original C1 — and it puts the 17e on the same modem as the rest of the [iPhone 17 line](/devices/iphone/17/).

However, the 17e is the only iPhone 17 model without the [N1](/chips/n1/) — Apple's Wi-Fi 7, Bluetooth 6 and Thread chip — and the [U2](/chips/u2/) ultra-wideband chip. The 17, 17 Air, 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max all carry both.
