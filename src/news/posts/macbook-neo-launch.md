The [MacBook Neo](/devices/mac/macbook-neo/) shipped today with the [A18 Pro](/chips/a18/pro/), making it the only Mac not built around an M-series chip.

It uses a variant that exists nowhere else. The [6c CPU / 6c GPU A18 Pro](/chips/a18/pro/6-6/) is the part that shipped in the [iPhone 16 Pro](/devices/iphone/16/pro/) and [16 Pro Max](/devices/iphone/16/pro-max/) in September 2024; the MacBook Neo gets a [6c CPU / 5c GPU](/chips/a18/pro/6-5/), one GPU core fewer.

The rest of the chip is what we've already seen in an iPhone 16: 16 Neural Engine cores and 35 TOPS, and **8 GB of memory at 60 GB/s** — against a 16 GB floor and 153.6 GB/s on the [M5](/chips/m5/) in the [MacBook Air 13″](/devices/mac/macbook-air-13/).
